### 📐 Models → ⚙️ Systems


| Model | What it answers | Feeds into |
|---|---|---|
| **Frontier Lab** | Which portfolio gives the best return for its risk? Mean-variance and risk-parity from shrinkage covariance, with VaR and expected shortfall | — |
| **Duration Desk** | How much does a bond book lose if rates shift or twist? Duration, convexity and key-rate shocks | — |
| **ReserveCast** | How much is owed on claims not yet reported? Chain-ladder and Bornhuetter-Ferguson with confidence bands | **Reconcilio** · payment reconciliation |
| **Hazard Lab** | How long until a claim is paid, denied or reopened? Kaplan-Meier and Cox models with censoring | **ClaimFlow** · claims rules engine |
